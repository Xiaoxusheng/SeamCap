The craft.

Four disciplines behind every SHIRUN program — from the mold that forms the shell to the carton that ships it.

Molded, not sewn.

A seamless cap begins as a single shell formed on a mold — no stitched panels, no crown seams, nothing to interrupt the line. The shell keeps its shape through wear and washing, and the silhouette stays clean from every angle.

One-piece construction is the core of our headwear range: solid colours, camo prints and open-top visors all start from the same discipline.

02 / Logo Application

Branding detail from our production partners.

Your mark, applied quietly.

Logos are applied according to material and artwork — embroidery, print or heat transfer — with placement, size and thread colours confirmed on a pre-production sample before bulk.

Colourway matching, woven labels and private-label packaging are coordinated in the same step, so branding decisions never delay production.

Standards, written down.

Before bulk production, specifications are confirmed with the selected production partner: measurements, materials, stitching-free zones, colour fastness expectations and inspection points.

During production, follow-up keeps the order aligned to the approved sample — because a luxury result is a process, not a promise.

Packed to travel.

Visors fold; caps hold their form. Packing is specified per program — polybag, carton count, label placement — so products arrive shelf-ready at their destination.

Export documents and shipment details are coordinated with the production partner, closing the loop from mold to market.
