Privacy policy.

Last updated: October 2026. This policy describes how Yiwu Shirun Headwear (“SHIRUN”, “we”) handles information you choose to share with us.

1. What we collect

When you contact us, you may choose to share: your name, company name, email address, WhatsApp or phone number, product interest, estimated quantity, customization requirements and your message. We collect only what you provide voluntarily.

2. How we use it

Enquiry information is used solely to respond to your request — answering questions, preparing quotations, arranging samples and following up on your order. We may add internal notes to your enquiry record so our team can serve you consistently. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

3. WhatsApp interactions

WhatsApp buttons on this website open a chat with our business number. We can see only the messages you choose to send us on WhatsApp. Clicking a WhatsApp button on this website is recorded, if at all, only as a click event — it does not mean a message was sent, and we cannot read your WhatsApp profile or conversations unless you message us first.

4. Enquiry form processing

Form submissions are transmitted to our server, validated to prevent abuse (including rate limiting and spam checks), and stored in our enquiry records. If email notification fails, your enquiry is still saved and reviewed. Submitting the form does not create any account or marketing profile.

5. Cookies & analytics

This website does not require accounts and sets no login cookies for public visitors. If we later add privacy-respecting statistics, this policy will be updated first.

6. Retention & your choices

Enquiry records are kept while relevant to your potential order and our legitimate business records. You may ask us to correct or delete your enquiry information at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp or via the enquiry form.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: WhatsApp +86 133 8861 4562, or use the contact form. Business address: Yiwu, Zhejiang, China.
